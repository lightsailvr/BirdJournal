Featherguild

Hear a bird, see its name. Bird identification for Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses and iPhone.

Featherguild listens through your glasses or your phone and names the birds singing around you, right on the lens. Add a bird to your journal with one tap, and every bird you add sews a collectible embroidered patch, with its song, call and facts.

Identification runs on your phone with the BirdNET model. It works offline, and the audio never leaves your device.

Guilds of friends

With an optional account you can join small, invite-only Guilds. Your finds reach your friends with the species, the city and the time, never an exact location. Friends react, compare their birds and chase monthly and custom goals together. If a friend sent you an invitation link, open it on your iPhone.

Free, with no ads

Every feature is free. If you'd like to help, an optional tip in the app goes toward the server. Tips unlock nothing.

Beta

Featherguild is in beta on TestFlight. Things will change, and some features described here are still being built.
