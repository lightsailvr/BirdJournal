You're invited to a Guild

A friend wants you in their Featherguild Guild, a small, invite-only group that sees each other's finds and chases goals together.

Have Featherguild? Open this link on your iPhone and it opens the app at the invitation. If it opened here instead, press and hold the link and choose "Open in Featherguild".

Not yet? Featherguild is a free iPhone app, in beta on TestFlight. Find out how to get it, install it, then open this link again on the same iPhone.

What joining means

You'll sign in with Apple or Google, which Featherguild needs only for Guilds. Members see the birds each other finds, with the species, the city and the time, never an exact location. You can leave a Guild at any time.

Invitations last seven days. If this one has expired, ask your friend for a new one.
