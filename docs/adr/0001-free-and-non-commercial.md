# Free and non-commercial, with tips and open-license pack photos

Status: accepted, 2026-10-13

The collection and circles were first imagined behind a paywall to recover hosting costs. We decided instead that BirdJournal stays free with every feature available, supported by optional in-app tips that unlock nothing. Charging for any part would make the whole app commercial use of its assets. About 97% of the pack photos are CC BY-NC (iNaturalist), about two-thirds of the reference sounds are CC BY-NC-SA (xeno-canto), and the BirdNET+ V3.0 preview's terms say "Provided solely for research and evaluation" even though its CC BY-SA 4.0 license allows commercial use.

Tips are a gray area under NonCommercial licenses, so pack photos move to open licenses only (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA). That costs a few hours in the pack builder, and nearly every species keeps photos: only six species across the five packs have no open-license photo. Reference sounds keep their NonCommercial clips, because only 20–30 species per pack have both an open song and an open call. No card art or derived data may come from a NonCommercial asset or a non-commercially licensed model.

## Consequences

- Any future paid feature reopens this ADR. The sounds would need the same open-license switch, the BirdNET team would need to grant permission, and the art model's license would need rechecking.
- eBird and Birds of the World data stay out: their terms are non-commercial and forbid redistribution or scraping.
