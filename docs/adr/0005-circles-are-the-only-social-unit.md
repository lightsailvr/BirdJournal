# Circles are the only social unit

Status: accepted, 2026-10-13

There are no friend lists, follows or public profiles. A friend is anyone who shares a circle with you, and a one-to-one friendship is a circle of two. One model then carries membership, the feed, goals, pushes, mutes and removal. Invite-only links and QR codes avoid user search and discovery, which keeps the privacy surface and the App Review moderation work small. A circle holds up to 20 accounts, any member can invite, links expire after 7 days, the creator can remove members, and a member's past finds stay when they leave.

## Considered options

- **Mutual friends with one feed**: closer to "friends", but it needs search or friend codes and a second model for group goals.
- **Friends plus circles**: two graphs to keep consistent for pushes and privacy.
