# BirdJournal — design decisions (grill sessions, 2026-09-26)

Source of truth for choices made on top of `spec.md`. Where this file and the spec disagree, this file wins.

## Product
- Glasses-first. The lens is the primary UI; the phone gets a minimal screen in phase D and social features later (out of scope).
- Personal project, published personally. Only the latest iOS is supported: deployment target iOS 27, Swift 6 language mode, Xcode 27.
- Android: out of scope. Web-app path: dropped (Web Apps have no mic or camera).

## Toolkit facts that shape the design
- Ambient audio only arrives in-band on a camera stream (`StreamConfiguration(audioCodec: .pcm(...))`). HFP is 8 kHz and beamformed to the wearer's voice; not usable.
- Camera audio, Inputs, photo capture, motion, speech and voice invocations are experimental in SDK 1.0.0: Dev Mode and Beta channel only.
- Real glasses do not deliver the Back event to apps (DAT 1.0.0 Inputs docs, "Current limitations": the on-glasses path is not wired yet; `consumeBack` only matters on the mock). The Neural Band's middle-finger tap is the system's back gesture, and since every view an app sends is the root as far as the glasses know, it ends the display session from any card (seen on hardware, #24). The app must give its own way back: swipe right on a card returns to the list. The two-finger temple tap also ends the session.
- An app that subscribes to Inputs receives Nav and Select itself; the glasses neither moved focus between tappable rows on the species list nor scrolled a tall card (#24, on hardware; the Inputs docs: subscribing "avoids consuming interactions that the system or on-glasses display would otherwise handle"). So a list is a menu the app drives: it keeps the selection, highlights it and re-sends; and long content is paged by the app. A Display button click still arrives (the details page's "Add to my list").
- Display: one root `FlexBox` per `send`, 600×600 additive display, dims and sleeps on inactivity. Images from bundled `UIImage` work offline. The docs say a tall view scrolls vertically, but with Inputs attached the glasses hand swipe up/down to the app and do not scroll (2026-09-27, #24), and the mock preview anchors overflow at the bottom. So every send fits the canvas and the app pages: a species has a photo page and a details page.
- A bundled `UIImage` is laid out at its pixel size on the 600-pixel canvas (measured on the mock in #7, to verify on hardware): a 600 px crop fills the card and pushes the text off it. Lens crops are 552 × 368 (3:2): the card's width under 24 px of padding, short enough that the name stays on the first screenful (#24; the 260 px square of #7 sat beside the name and left half the canvas blank).
- Only one third-party app can be registered in Dev Mode at a time. Bundle IDs may not contain `-`.
- One device session per device (`DeviceSessionError.sessionAlreadyExists`). The listening run (#9) starts one session and shares it between the camera stream, Display and Inputs; the single-capability screens and mock tests still start their own.
- Display 1.0.0 has no wake call. The docs say the display dims after 20 s and sleeps at 25 s without ending the session; the app re-sends the current card (the species list or the species card with its refreshed status strip) on each new species, and whether that wakes a sleeping display is checked on hardware (#9, #24).
- MockDeviceKit cannot inject audio frames; sound-ID tests run on recorded files. With a still-image feed it delivers no video frames either (#9), so the frame path is tested with a stand-in source and the real stream's frames on hardware.

## Audio and identification
- Glasses stream: camera at `.low` resolution, lowest frame rate, audio PCM mono. Sample rate 44.1 kHz (chosen in the phase A spike), resampled to the model rate.
- Video frames are not used for identification. The most recent frame at the moment of the "Add to my list" tap (#24; it was the confirm tap in #9) is stored with the sighting as a JPEG under Application Support/Frames, referenced by a relative path; the camera-assist re-ranker is out of scope.
- Model: BirdNET+ V3.0 preview 3.1 (11,560 species, 32 kHz input, CC BY-SA 4.0 weights, "Powered by BirdNET" attribution). Pinned to the exact Zenodo file. Engine sits behind a protocol so v2.4 can be swapped in.
- Geo prior: BirdNET geomodel v3.0.4 (Apache-2.0), raw lat/lon/week input, used as a pre-filter with the reference threshold 0.03. No GBIF/H3 tables in v1.
- Runtime: ONNX Runtime via the official Swift package (`microsoft/onnxruntime-swift-package-manager`), consumed through the fork `lightsailvr/onnxruntime-swift-package-manager` so Xcode Cloud can fetch it. Core ML conversion is a later optimization, not a dependency.
- Audio source is a protocol with three implementations: glasses stream, phone mic, WAV file (tests).
- Windows: 3 s with 1.5 s overlap; a species enters the stack after two windows above threshold. Thresholds tuned in phase B.
- Ring buffer exists only for live playback. No audio is saved to the album.
- Location: "when in use" permission; one fix at session start, refresh every 10 min; no background location mode.
- Background: the app must keep listening with the phone locked (audio + processing + Bluetooth background modes). Verified in phase A.

## Lens UI
- Gestures: swipe left/right = Nav, swipe up/down = Nav too (selection on the list, photo ⇄ details on a species; the glasses do not scroll for an app that subscribes to Inputs), tap = Select. Middle-finger tap and two-finger tap = quit (system; Back never reaches the app on real glasses).
- Page map (#24, replaces the #7 photo/description/confirm/saved pages): the root is the **species list** ("N species heard", one row per species heard, newest last: name, match rate, a photo icon when the pack has a card, a check when added), driven as a menu: swipe down/up move the highlighted row, a tap or swipe left opens it. The list is paged five rows at a time around the selection (a re-send starts at the top and the glasses do not scroll); the hint line says which page. Two pages per species: the **photo page** (the photo first and full width, the common name with the match rate beside it, the status strip "4 species · 2 of 4" with "· Added" once added, and the hint "Swipe down for more information · swipe right: all species"; a tap does nothing, so no bird is added by accident) and, on swipe down, the **details page** (name and match rate, field marks, size · habitat, the photo credit and the **"Add to my list"** primary button, or "Added to my list ✓"). Swipe up returns to the photo. On either page swipe left is the next species' photo and **swipe right is back to the list** (the only way back, since Back never reaches the app), with that species selected. Every page is one screenful inside the word budget.
- "Add to my list" saves in one action: the sighting is written with the camera frame from the moment of the tap. Both a Display button click and an Inputs Select on the details page save. A press delivered both ways (the open question from #7/#9) is two saves of one species within a second, which the adapter drops as an echo; a later tap on an added species' details page saves again and updates the sighting (spec user story 33). There is no confirm page. On the list, an Inputs Select opens the highlighted row.
- Stack never reorders; new species append at the end. A new species changes the status strip and the list but never moves the wearer off their card and never sends them to the root. Back from the list ends the session; Back from a card returns to the list.
- Photo-first cards: close-up crops with dark backgrounds preferred. Text ≤ ~40 words per **screenful** (the card's first screenful is strip, photo, name and match rate; the second is the text, credit and button); the list splits its rows into screenfuls the same way.
- Settled on hardware (#24): the glasses neither scroll a tall view nor move focus between tappable rows while the app subscribes to Inputs (swipes reach the app instead); the middle-finger tap ends the session (Back never reaches the app). Still to verify (DAT-SETUP-CHECKLIST.md): whether Select on the details page's button arrives as a click, an Inputs select or both (both add, deduplicated).
- Audio chirp on new candidate: off by default.
- Session start: phone button in v1; "Hey Meta, start BirdJournal" once the Developer Center approves Voice Invocation.

## Species pack
- Region v1: Los Angeles area, ~150 species with photos and descriptions. All BirdNET species remain identifiable by name.
- LA pack bundled in the app binary (offline on first launch). Other packs downloaded from GitHub Releases via a JSON index into app storage.
- Pack = zip of SQLite + pre-sized JPEGs (lens 552 × 368, see the Display image-size fact above; phone ~1200 px). A bird too tall for the card-shaped crop keeps its head and loses its tail.
- Pack builder (Python) adds a detector step (COCO "bird") to crop close-ups, scores crops on bird area, background luminance and sharpness, keeps top 3–5 per species, with a manual override list.
- Photo licenses: CC0, CC BY and CC BY-NC. Every photo carries observer, license and source URL. Credit shown on the species card and in a full credits screen on the phone.
- Pack builder facts (#8): the iNaturalist API is the default metadata source (the Open Data dump is tens of GB; the DuckDB path over it exists for full rebuilds), ordered newest first because the most-faved observations are the oddities (every top-voted California Towhee was leucistic). Detector: COCO SSD MobileNet v1 (ONNX zoo, Apache-2.0); it misses birds under about 5 % of the frame, which are poor cards anyway. Crops below 0.4 sharpness are dropped before ranking; hand-held and dead birds still need the override file. The ten-species pack (about 9 MB) is committed under `packs/`; when #12 grows it, move it to a GitHub Release fetched like the models.

## Phase A go/no-go (issue #3) — GO, 2026-09-26
Run: glasses source, 44.1 kHz mono, `.low` at 2 fps, iPhone 17 Pro locked in a pocket, 19 min 45 s (`spike-20260926-193507.log`).
- Sample rate: **44.1 kHz**. 48 kHz is untested: the first attempt stalled on the Meta AI microphone grant, not on the rate.
- Coverage: 1,182.0 s of audio in 1,185.2 s of wall time (99.7 %; the shortfall is stream startup). Zero gaps over 2 s. Chunks every 23.2 ms on average (1,024 samples); the longest interval between chunks was 671 ms.
- Latency above best: 39–89 ms mean per 30 s interval, 689 ms worst. No upward drift over the run, so the link does not back up. Absolute glasses latency is unknown: presentation times are not on the phone's host clock (first offset 882,188 s).
- Glasses battery 100 → 88 % (about 0.6 %/min, so roughly 2.5–3 h of listening from full). Glasses thermal `none` throughout. Phone battery 65 → 65 %, thermal `nominal`.
- Background: status lines kept arriving every 30 s while locked (`background_locked` for 37 of 40 intervals). The Bluetooth and external-accessory modes are enough; no `AVAudioSession` is needed on the glasses path.
- One `stream_error "Critical error, the stream should end"` arrived 2 s after the app went to the background, but the stream kept `streaming` and audio never stopped. Watch for it; do not stop the source on stream errors alone.
- The first audio chunk after `streaming` was empty (0 samples); consumers must tolerate empty chunks.
- Doff does **not** pause (`spike-20260926-195716.log`): 12 s after taking the glasses off, the device ended the session (`session_error "Session ended by device"`, session and stream `stopped`) and the link dropped. After putting them back on, the glasses were `donned` and `connected` within about 35 s, but the session stayed stopped and no audio returned. The app must start a new session itself once the glasses are worn and connected again; waiting for `.paused` → `.started` never happens. Touchpad-tap pause is still untested.
- Verdict: **GO** for the audio path: continuous, survives a locked phone, and costs about 12 % glasses battery per 20 minutes. Follow-up needed: automatic session restart after doff/don (the listening session must survive taking the glasses off). Built in #10, see "Pause, disconnect and error handling".

## Pause, disconnect and error handling (issue #10) — built 2026-09-27, hardware run pending
- The listening run survives the glasses. Phase A showed a doff does not pause: the glasses end the session ("Session ended by device") and drop the link, and walking out of range looks the same. So when a session stops on the device side the run reads the glasses' state to tell the cases apart: `donState == .doffed` is a doff, a link that is not `.connected` is a lost link, and glasses still worn and connected for a 2 s grace period (a link drop can be reported a beat after the session ends) is the wearer quitting (two-finger or middle-finger tap), which ends the run as before.
- A doff or a lost link **pauses** the run instead of ending it: the lens releases Display and Inputs but keeps its pages, selection and saved marks (`GlassesLensSession.Phase.suspended`); the audio source drops the camera but keeps the engine's chunk stream open (the engine sees a silence, not an end, so the stack survives); the phone shows "Paused: glasses off" or "Paused: glasses disconnected" with Stop as the way out. The run then waits, as long as it takes, for the glasses to be connected and not doffed, starts a new session itself, puts the same pages back on the lens and continues the stream on it (three tries, 5 s apart; then the run ends failed and the Start button is the restart). Nothing restarts from the phone.
- A session the toolkit pauses (`.paused`, the touchpad; the mock's `captouch.tap()` does this) is shown on the phone as "Paused by the glasses" and resumes with the session; the lens re-sends the current card in case the display dropped it. Untested on hardware.
- A session that ends with a thermal, battery, peak-power, glasses-app-update or SDK-version error ends the run failed rather than waiting: the glasses will not restart it until the wearer does something.
- Problems the wearer should know about while the lens is reachable are a **problem page** (`LensPage.problem`) over whatever page they were on: "No location" when the first fix is denied or does not arrive (identification carries on without the regional filter, as decided in #6), and "Connection lost" as the first card after a dropped link comes back. Swipe right, Back or a tap is the way back to the covered page; the other swipes stay; species heard meanwhile append behind it. A doff and don shows no page: it was the wearer's own doing. The phone mirrors the card and shows the location state.
- Glasses that leave again during a resume are waited for again. Stop from the phone in any active phase (listening, deciding, paused, reconnecting) cancels the reconnection and tears down the lens, engine, stream and session the same way; every listener token lives in a bag that is emptied on every suspension and stop, so three start-stop cycles leave nothing behind (mock test; the run deallocates after).
- Verified on the mock: doff + power off then power on + don resumes the pages and the audio without a restart; power off while worn is a lost link and the return shows "Connection lost" with swipe right back; the touchpad pause and resume; Stop while paused; no location; three cycles. To verify on the glasses (DAT-SETUP-CHECKLIST.md): the real doff/don timings, walking out of range and back, the touchpad, and that a quit still reads "Ended by the glasses" after the grace period.

## Phase C end to end (issue #9) — built 2026-09-27, hardware run pending
_The confirm page and Save button described here were replaced by the species details page's "Add to my list" action in #24 (see "Lens UI"); the run, the album write and the ending paths are unchanged._
- "Listen with the glasses" on the phone runs the whole loop: one device session, camera stream at 44.1 kHz mono / `.low` / 2 fps through the engine, the lens pages over the same session, tap to confirm, Save to SwiftData. Start order: session, permissions, lens (so "Listening" shows while the models load), then the stream and engine.
- Confirming a species already saved in the run updates that sighting (latest confidence, new frame if there is one) instead of adding a second, so the album stays clean (spec user story 33). A Session record and the doff/don pause are not part of this issue.
- Ending: Stop on the phone, Back on the listening page (once hardware delivers Back), or the glasses ending the session (two-finger tap, doff, link loss) all tear down lens, engine, stream and session the same way. _Since #10 a doff or a lost link pauses the run instead; see "Pause, disconnect and error handling"._
- Verified on the mock: audio (stand-in source) to lens to a saved Sighting with species, time, location, confidence and source; the frame stored is the one from confirm time. To verify on the glasses: card within 6 s of a bird call, confirm and Save with the phone in a pocket, a real camera frame in the sighting, and the Listening page waking the display on a new species.
