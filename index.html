Bird Journal

Hear a bird, see its name. Bird identification for Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses and iPhone.

Bird Journal listens through your glasses or your phone and names the birds singing around you, right on the lens. Add a bird to your journal with one tap, and every sighting becomes a collectible card with the bird's song, call, facts and painted art.

Identification runs on your phone with the BirdNET model. It works offline, and the audio never leaves your device.

Free, with no ads

Every feature is free. If you'd like to help, an optional tip in the app goes toward the server and the machine that paints the cards. Tips unlock nothing.

Beta

Bird Journal is in beta on TestFlight. Things will change, and some features described here are still being built.
